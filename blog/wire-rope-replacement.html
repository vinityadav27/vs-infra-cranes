When to Replace Wire Rope on Your Hoist

Jun 05, 2024•4 min read

Wire rope failure can be catastrophic. Learn the visible signs of wear, broken wires, and diameter reduction that indicate it's time for an immediate replacement per IS standards.

The Lifeline of Your Crane

The wire rope is arguably the most critical load-bearing component of any electric wire rope hoist. Operating with a degraded rope is a massive safety hazard. According to IS standard guidelines, wire ropes must be inspected daily.

Signs It's Time for Replacement

1. Broken Wires: If you spot multiple broken wires within a single strand or across the rope's lay length, the rope's structural integrity is compromised. Replace it immediately.

2. Diameter Reduction: Over time, the core of the rope can deteriorate, causing a noticeable reduction in the rope's overall diameter. Measure the diameter regularly; a reduction of more than 5% is a red flag.

3. Kinking or Crushing: Any physical deformation, such as kinking, crushing, or bird-caging, means the rope must be retired. These deformations create stress points that will inevitably fail.

Conclusion
