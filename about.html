About VS Infra & Cranes

A legacy of engineering excellence in industrial crane manufacturing since 2021.

Our Story

From Humble Beginnings to Industry Leaders

Founded in 2021 in Faridabad, Haryana, VS Infra & Cranes was born from a vision to provide world-class crane solutions to Indian industries. What began as a small workshop with a team of dedicated engineers has grown into one of the region's most trusted names in industrial crane manufacturing.

Over the years, we expanded our manufacturing facility to a modern, 50,000 sq. ft. plant equipped with CNC machines, precision welding stations, and an in-house testing laboratory. Every crane that leaves our facility undergoes rigorous quality checks and load testing.

Today, with 500+ successful installations across diverse industries and 200+ satisfied clients, we continue to push the boundaries of crane engineering — delivering solutions that are safer, more efficient, and built to last.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, safe, and efficient crane and material handling solutions that empower industries to operate at their full potential — backed by engineering expertise and unwavering service commitment.

Our Vision

To be India's most trusted name in industrial crane manufacturing, known for engineering excellence, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to safety and long-term customer partnership.
